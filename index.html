Practical info

  • Arrival at 00:30. The airport train into Rome is not running at that hour. The simplest option is a taxi on the fixed fare into the city walls, around €55 (to be confirmed), or a private transfer. Agree late check-in with the host.
  • Departure on Sunday. Leave the Airbnb by 15:30 at the latest. The Leonardo Express from Termini to Fiumicino takes about 32 minutes.
  • Weather. November in Rome is cool and often rainy. Bring an umbrella and layers.
  • Vatican and churches. Shoulders and knees covered.
  • Water. The street fountains (nasoni) give free drinking water.
  • Pickpockets. Keep an eye on bags at the Colosseum, on the metro and at the Trevi Fountain.
